Frequently asked questions

Do I need to drill holes?

No. It mounts with an adhesive plate. Clean and dry the wall first for the best hold.

Does the UV light replace washing my brush?

No. Rinse your toothbrush as normal. The UV light is an extra feature, not a medical or disinfecting claim.

How is it powered?

It has a solar panel to assist the battery. Please message us if you need details on charging for your version.

Will it fit my electric toothbrush?

It fits many standard handles. Very thick handles may not fit, so check the 4.7 cm depth against your brush.
