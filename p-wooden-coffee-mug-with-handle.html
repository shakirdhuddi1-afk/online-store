Frequently asked questions

Can I put it in the dishwasher or microwave?

No. Wooden mugs should be hand washed with mild soap, dried straight away and kept out of the microwave.

Will my mug look exactly like the photo?

Wood grain and shade vary, so your mug will have its own unique pattern.

Is it good for hot drinks?

Wood does not get as hot to hold as metal or glass, but let very hot drinks cool for a moment.

What size is it?

About 9 cm wide at the top and 10 cm tall.
